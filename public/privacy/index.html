Official Data Protection & Privacy Practices

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Official Privacy Statement of EarnFlow.in

01 Platform Overview & Privacy Commitment

Welcome to EarnFlow.in (https://earnflow.in). EarnFlow.in is an online gaming platform providing users with access to interactive games, gaming events, account management, and USDT-based payment features.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used and protected, and your rights regarding your data when you visit our website, register an account, or play games on EarnFlow.in.

This Privacy Policy is publicly accessible without requiring registration or login.

02 Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information strictly required to deliver our gaming and payment features:

  • Registration & Profile Data: Email address, player username, and optional profile avatar.
  • Google OAuth Authentication Data: When authenticating via Google Sign-In, we receive your Google User ID, primary email address, verified email status, and public profile name and photo.
  • Public TRC-20 Wallet Address: Your public TRON blockchain address used for deposits and prize disbursals. We never request, access, or store your private keys or recovery passphrases.
  • Gaming & Provably Fair Records: Event participation, game rounds, ticket numbers, bet amounts, cryptographic seeds, and prize payouts.
  • Financial Transaction Ledger: USDT wallet balances, deposit invoice identifiers, on-chain transaction hashes (TxHash), and withdrawal records.
  • Technical Metadata: IP address, device type, browser user-agent, operating system, and session timestamps used strictly for security monitoring and DDoS defense.

03 Google Sign-In & OAuth 2.0 Practices

EarnFlow.in provides Google Sign-In to allow users to sign up and authenticate quickly and securely without storing passwords on our servers.

Information Obtained from Google Authentication:

  • Google Profile ID: Unique numeric identifier.
  • Email Address: Used for account identification and important security alerts.
  • Verified Email Status: Confirmation that your email address is authentic.
  • Display Name & Profile Picture: Populates your public player handle.

Strictly Limited Scope: We do NOT access your Google Drive files, Gmail messages, contact lists, calendar, browsing history, or Google account password.

Our use of Google user data adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

04 How Personal Information Is Used

We use the collected information for the following explicit purposes:

  • Authentication & Account Access: Verifying your identity and securing your account sessions.
  • Delivering Gaming Features: Powering interactive games (Four-Digit Lottery, Crash, Dice, Coinflip, Bingo – Color Trading, and Horse Racing) and generating provably fair random results.
  • USDT Payments & Prize Disbursals: Managing wallet balances, crediting deposits, calculating winnings, and processing withdrawals in USDT.
  • Fair Play Auditing: Recording SHA-256 pre-commit seeds so every round can be independently verified.
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting duplicate accounts, bot exploitation, and maintaining platform security.
  • Customer Support: Responding to user inquiries submitted to support@earnflow.in.

05 Third-Party Service Providers & Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties or advertisers.

We share information only with trusted infrastructure providers necessary for operational execution:

  • Google LLC: For OAuth 2.0 authentication and identity verification.
  • Supabase Inc.: Encrypted cloud database infrastructure with Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcement.
  • NOWPayments & TRON Blockchain: Non-custodial gateway for TRC-20 USDT deposit invoice generation. Transactions recorded on the public TRON blockchain are transparent by nature of distributed ledger technology.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly required by a court order, enforceable subpoena, or applicable statutory law.

06 Data Storage, Protection & Security Practices

We employ robust enterprise-grade safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted is protected with TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database storage and backups are encrypted using AES-256.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): PostgreSQL RLS policies restrict users to accessing only their own private wallet, ticket, and profile data.
  • HMAC Authentication: Cryptographic signatures verify backend edge operations against tampering.

07 Cookies and Local Storage

EarnFlow.in utilizes browser LocalStorage solely for operational necessities: storing authentication session tokens (JWT) and user interface preferences (such as audio mute and sidebar toggles). We do not deploy third-party advertising or retargeting cookies.

08 User Rights: Access, Correction & Deletion

You have full sovereign rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request an export of your account and transaction ledger data.
  • Correction: Modify your username or linked TRC-20 address directly in your Profile.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and personal data by emailing support@earnflow.in. Account deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 days.
  • Revoke Google Access: Revoke permissions anytime via your Google Account Security page.

09 Data Retention Policy

We retain personal data for the duration of your active account. If you request account closure, personal identifiers are deleted within 30 days, retaining only immutable blockchain transaction hashes and legal audit records required by applicable regulations.

10 Age Restrictions & Protection of Minors

EarnFlow.in is strictly intended for individuals who are at least 18 years of age (or legal age of majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. Any discovered account belonging to a minor will be terminated immediately.
